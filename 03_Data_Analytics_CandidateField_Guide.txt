DATA DICTIONARY – SALES TRANSACTIONS

transaction_id: Transaction identifier. Duplicate identifiers have been deliberately included.
transaction_date: Date of transaction; date formats may be inconsistent.
customer: Customer or organization name; some values may be missing.
region: Sales region; some values may be missing or inconsistently formatted.
product: Product sold.
quantity: Units sold; invalid values may be present.
unit_price_ngn: Unit price in Nigerian naira; missing values may be present.
discount_rate: Discount as a decimal (e.g., 0.10 means 10%); invalid values may be present.
payment_method: Payment channel; category labels may be inconsistent.
sales_rep: Sales representative; some values may be missing.
order_status: Completed, Pending or Cancelled; category labels may be inconsistent.

Suggested calculation for a valid completed order:
net_sales = quantity * unit_price_ngn * (1 - discount_rate)

This is a suggested definition, not a requirement. Candidates should state and justify their assumptions. Do not include cancelled or pending orders in completed-sales revenue unless explicitly explaining a separate metric.
